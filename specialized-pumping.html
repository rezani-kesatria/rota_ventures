Product Supports

Specialized Pumping.

Precision-engineered pumps for the fluids that defeat standard equipment — high-viscosity, corrosive, abrasive and extreme-temperature media, moved reliably.

The Problem

Standard pumps die here.

The fluid always wins

Aggressive chemicals eat seals, abrasive slurries scour internals, viscous media stall impellers, and heat softens what the chemical didn't. A catalogue pump on the wrong fluid isn't a maintenance problem — it's a countdown.

How We Solve

The pump is built around your fluid.

Matched to the fluid, not the catalogue

Progressive cavity, diaphragm, peristaltic and gear pumps — each custom-configured to your fluid's properties, operating conditions and duty. Materials and seals selected for compatibility before price.

The Specification

The hard duty, handled daily.

Wearresistant

Extreme durability

Materials and seals selected for maximum chemical compatibility and wear resistance — the pump outlasts the fluid's best attempts to destroy it.

0leaks tolerated

Sealed and safe

Sealed designs prevent leaks of hazardous materials, and proven designs minimise downtime in processes where a release is not an option.

Transferto dosing

Flow you can set

Precise flow control improves product quality — metering duties from transfer to dosing, steady against viscosity and pressure swings.

Applications

Industries that push their luck.

Chemical & pharmaceutical

Corrosive media and contamination-free duties, where the seal package matters as much as the pump.

Mining & refining

Food, paint & pulp
