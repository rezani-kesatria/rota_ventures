Product Supports

Specialized Pumping.

Precision-engineered pumps for the fluids that defeat standard equipment — high-viscosity, corrosive, abrasive and extreme-temperature media, moved reliably.

The Problem

Standard pumps die here.

The fluid always wins

Aggressive chemicals eat seals, abrasive slurries scour internals, viscous media stall impellers, and heat softens what the chemical didn't. A catalogue pump on the wrong fluid isn't a maintenance problem — it's a countdown.

Matched to the fluid, not the catalogue

Progressive cavity, diaphragm, peristaltic and gear pumps — each custom-configured to your fluid's properties, operating conditions and duty. Materials and seals selected for compatibility before price.

The Outcome

The hard duty, handled daily.

Delivery

From fluid sample to spec'd pump.

01

Application consultation

It starts with the fluid, not the pump.

  • Fluid properties, solids content and temperature range documented
  • Process duty, head and flow profile established
  • Failure history of the current installation reviewed
02

Compatibility testing

Materials face the fluid before the pump does.

  • Elastomer and metallurgy coupons against the process media
  • Wear testing for abrasive and shear-sensitive duties
  • Selection documented with margins, not guesses
03

Custom configuration

One of four pump families, configured to the duty.

  • Progressive cavity for viscous and shear-sensitive fluids
  • Diaphragm for slurries and corrosives; peristaltic for contamination-free duty
  • Gear pumps for precision metering at high pressure
04

Field service & commissioning

Installed, aligned and proven on the real line.

  • Installation, alignment and dry-run checks
  • Troubleshooting against the live process
  • Operating envelope handed to the crew
05

Life-cycle support

Rebuilds and spares keep the hard duty covered.

  • Rebuild and refurbishment programs
  • Comprehensive spare parts inventory
  • Condition reviews timed to the fluid's wear pattern

Applications

Industries that push their luck.

Chemical & pharmaceutical

Corrosive media and contamination-free duties, where the seal package matters as much as the pump.

Mining & refining

Slurries, solvents and petroleum streams that score, gum and dissolve ordinary internals.

Food, paint & pulp

Viscous, shear-sensitive and fibrous media moved gently, precisely and without stopping the line.
