Engineering Solutions

Combined Heat & Power.

Electricity and useful heat from one fuel, engineered to global standards for safety, reliability and maintenance efficiency — recovering up to 80% of the energy you already pay for.

The Problem

You buy energy twice, and lose most of it once.

Two bills, one process

The grid sells you electricity for the motors. Then fuel is bought for the boilers to raise the heat the process needs anyway. In separate heat and power, most of that fuel's energy never reaches the product — it leaves the stack as rejected heat.

One fuel, two outputs

A CHP plant generates electricity on site and captures the engine's heat for process steam, hot water or drying. Same fuel, both outputs — up to 80% of its energy doing paid work, with generation engineered to global safety and reliability standards.

Delivery

From first audit to first megawatt.

01

Site assessment

Engineers audit the site's power and heat demand before anything is proposed.

  • Facility audit and electrical and thermal load profiling
  • Heat-demand mapping across the process
  • Integration points, fuel supply and site constraints
02

System design

A blueprint balancing power output, thermal recovery and fuel efficiency.

  • Sizing against measured electrical and thermal profiles
  • Equipment selection and leasing structure recommendations
  • Cost, emissions and payback modelling
03

Installation & integration

Deployment ties into existing electrical and thermal infrastructure without stopping production.

  • Coordinated logistics and equipment delivery
  • Installation by certified engineers
  • Connection to live electrical and thermal systems in planned phases
04

Commissioning & testing

Performance is validated against specification and safety standards before handover.

  • Safety and standards verification
  • Load and heat-recovery performance testing
  • Handover documentation and operator training
05

Ongoing optimisation

Monitoring and predictive maintenance hold efficiency at design levels for the asset's life.

  • Continuous performance monitoring
  • Predictive maintenance and after-sales support
  • Tuning to demand, load and fuel changes

Applications

Where CHP earns its keep.

Process & manufacturing

Hospitals, campuses & data centres

Loads that need power plus heating or cooling around the clock, where outage risk carries a cost measured in more than ringgit.
