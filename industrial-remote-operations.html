Engineering Solutions

Industrial Remote Operations.

Legacy motor and piping infrastructure retrofitted with sensing, control and analytics — so the plant runs visible, optimised and safer from a single control point.

The Problem

Most plants learn the truth when it stops.

Running on walk-arounds

Legacy motors, pumps and piping report through clipboard and memory. Readings are hours old by the time they're logged, energy waste stays invisible, and the first true alarm is usually the machine already stopped.

The plant, live on one screen

Sensing, control and analytics are retrofitted onto the equipment you already run. Flow, vibration, temperature and power become live numbers — watched, logged and acted on from a single control point, on site or away.

The Outcome

The plant, finally legible.

Delivery

From walk-arounds to one screen.

01

Infrastructure assessment

We map the motors, pumps, piping and control panels you already run, and find what's worth instrumenting.

  • Asset and criticality audit across the plant
  • Existing PLC, panel and SCADA review
  • Baseline measurements of energy and throughput
02

Sensor deployment

Instrumentation goes onto the assets you already run — no replacement required.

  • Vibration, temperature, pressure and flow sensing
  • Non-invasive mounting wherever the asset allows
  • Coverage prioritised by criticality, not convenience
03

Network integration

A secure path carries readings from the plant floor to the monitoring platform.

  • Industrial network and communications setup
  • Encrypted transmission to the monitoring platform
  • Integration with existing SCADA or historians where present
04

Control upgrade

Legacy panels gain remote operation, alarms and modern diagnostics — enhanced, not replaced.

  • Control panel enhancement and interlock modernisation
  • Remote start, stop and setpoint control
  • Alarm management with escalation paths
05

Analytics & optimisation

Data becomes decisions: predictions, alerts and tuning against demand.

  • Predictive maintenance models on live readings
  • Energy and throughput optimisation
  • Performance reviews with your operations team

Applications

Built for brownfield.

Water & wastewater

Pump stations, treatment lines and distribution networks spread across more sites than any walk-round schedule can cover.

Oil, gas & chemical

Hazardous or remote process areas where every unnecessary human entry is a safety exposure and every surprise stoppage is a report.

Manufacturing & utilities

Motor-heavy lines and plant utilities where one stopped drive stops the whole shift, and energy waste compounds quietly.
