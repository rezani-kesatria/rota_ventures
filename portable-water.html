Product Supports

Portable Water & Wastewater.

Containerised treatment plants that travel — industrial-grade purification deployed wherever the work is, and gone when it isn't.

The Problem

The site moves. The water doesn't care.

No grid, no plant, no exceptions

Mining camps, construction sites and relief operations need drinking water and clean discharge from day one — usually where no infrastructure exists and no permanent plant can ever pay for itself.

A treatment plant in a box

Pre-engineered systems in 20' and 40' ISO containers arrive tested and ready to commission — filtration, UV, reverse osmosis and biological treatment complete with pumps, controls and monitoring.

The Outcome

Water standards, without the construction.

Delivery

From source survey to first clean litre.

01

Water assessment

Source and demand are profiled before a container is configured.

  • Raw water quality analysis at the source
  • Demand profiling for potable and process loads
  • Discharge requirements and local standards review
02

Container configuration

A treatment train matched to the water, packed into ISO boxes.

  • Process selection across filtration, UV, RO and biological treatment
  • Sizing from 10 to 500 m³/day against measured demand
  • Pumps, controls and monitoring integrated at the factory
03

Delivery & siting

The plant ships as freight and lands as infrastructure.

  • Logistics to remote sites, including generator-compatible power setup
  • On-site installation and connection by our engineers
  • Operator training programs before first use
04

Commissioning

From power-on to compliant water inside 24 to 48 hours.

  • System flush, dosing and calibration
  • Water quality verification against drinking and discharge standards
  • Handover with monitoring baselines recorded
05

Operation & support

The plant stays compliant for as long as the site runs.

  • Regular maintenance and consumable supply
  • Water quality testing and reporting
  • 24/7 emergency response team on call

Applications

Wherever the map runs out.

Mining camps & construction

Potable water for the crew and compliant discharge for the regulator, from ground-break to rehabilitation.

Disaster relief & military

Rapid-deployment purification for emergency response and field operations, generator-compatible and air-freightable.

Events, agriculture & remote industry

Temporary facilities, irrigation schemes and oil & gas exploration — treated water without permanent works.
