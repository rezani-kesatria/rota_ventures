Portable Water & Wastewater

Deployable treatment solutions engineered to provide reliable water management and filtration in remote or temporary industrial environments.

Description

Our portable water and wastewater treatment systems deliver industrial-grade purification wherever it's needed. Designed for rapid deployment and easy relocation, these containerized units provide complete water treatment capabilities for remote sites, emergency response, temporary facilities, and mobile operations. Each system is pre-engineered, tested, and ready for immediate commissioning upon arrival.

Category

Product Supports · Water Treatment · Portable Systems · Environmental Solutions

Technical Specifications

  • Treatment Capacity: 10 to 500 m³/day
  • Configuration: Containerized (20' or 40' ISO containers)
  • Treatment Processes: Filtration, UV, RO, biological treatment
  • Power Requirements: 230-480V, generator-compatible

Benefits

  • Rapid Deployment: Set up and operational within 24-48 hours of delivery
  • Flexible Mobility: Easily relocated as project sites change or expand
  • Complete Solution: All-in-one systems including pumps, controls, and monitoring
  • Regulatory Compliance: Meets drinking water and discharge standards
  • Cost-Effective: Avoid permanent infrastructure investment for temporary needs

Applications

Mining camps, construction sites, disaster relief, military operations, oil & gas exploration, temporary event facilities, agricultural operations, and remote industrial installations.

Support Services

  • On-site installation and commissioning
  • Operator training programs
  • 24/7 emergency response team
  • Regular maintenance and consumable supply
  • Water quality testing and reporting
