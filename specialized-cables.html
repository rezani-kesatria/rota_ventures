Product Supports

Specialized Cables.

High-durability cabling engineered to survive the mechanical stress, heat and chemical exposure that ends standard cable's life — keeping hazardous installations energised and instrumented.

The Problem

Standard insulation wasn't asked to live here.

The environment takes the weakest link

Heat embrittles insulation, solvents attack sheaths, abrasion wears through armour, and a single failed cable can stop a refinery unit or darken an offshore platform. Standard cable doesn't fail because it's bad — it fails because the zone is worse.

Cable built for the zone

Advanced insulation, reinforced construction and specialised shielding — matched per run to heat, chemicals, flexing and hazardous-area classification, so the cable outlasts everything around it.

The Specification

Rated for the worst day.

5–10×

Lifespan over standard

In harsh environments these cables outlast standard product five to ten times — fewer replacements, fewer outage windows, lower long-term cost.

250°C continuous

Heat, rated and held

High-temperature constructions run continuously at up to 250°C, beside furnaces and on hot process lines where ordinary insulation gives up.

ATEX· IECEx

Certified for the zone

Explosion-proof types carry hazardous-area certifications, with armour and shielding for mechanical protection and signal integrity. Pair with remote monitoring.

Delivery

From run list to certified circuit.

01

Application review

Every run is classified before it is specified.

  • Route, load and environment surveyed per circuit
  • Hazardous-area classification and code requirements confirmed
  • Failure history of the existing installation reviewed
02

Selection & custom build

The construction matches the threat.

  • High-temperature, chemical-resistant, armoured, explosion-proof or flexible types — chosen per run
  • Custom configuration and manufacturing to the route's exact lengths
  • Shielding specified where signal integrity matters
03

Installation guidance

A good cable pulled badly is a bad cable.

  • Routing, gland and bend-radius guidance for the crew
  • Training for site electricians on termination practice
  • Supervision at the hazardous-area interface
04

Testing & certification

The circuit enters service with paperwork, not hope.

  • Insulation, continuity and shield testing per circuit
  • Certification aligned to hazardous-area requirements
  • Test records handed to the asset register
05

Emergency stock & support

When a cable fails anyway, the shelf is ready.

  • Emergency replacement stock programs held against critical runs
  • Rapid re-supply of custom builds
  • Periodic condition reviews of the installed base

Applications

Where standard cable doesn't come back.

Refineries & offshore platforms

Chemical attack, weather and explosion risk in one location — cable specified to the zone classification, not the catalogue.

Mines & steel mills

Abrasion, crush loads and furnace heat — armoured and high-temperature builds that keep moving equipment moving.

Power & hazardous zones

Generation facilities and classified areas where one cable fault is an outage, a safety event, or both.
