Product Supports

Economical Pumping & Compression.

Cost-efficient pumping and compression that cuts power consumption, not pressure — engineered for buyers whose decision hinges on total cost of ownership.

The Problem

Most pumps burn power no one signed off on.

Fixed speed, wasted energy

Fixed-speed pumps meet varying demand by throttling — fighting their own output with valves and bypass loops. The electricity goes into friction instead of flow, and the waste is buried in a power bill nobody audits line by line.

How We Solve

The fix is output that follows demand.

Efficiency as a specification

Variable frequency drives, high-efficiency motors and optimised hydraulics let output follow demand — power is spent on the fluid, not on fighting the system, and the saving shows up on the invoice every month.

The Specification

What economy looks like on paper.

30–50%

Less power, same output

Variable frequency drives and high-efficiency motors cut power consumption by 30–50% against fixed-speed systems — without sacrificing pressure or reliability.

IE4/IE5

Motor class, specified

Motors specified up to IE4/IE5 efficiency with hydraulics matched to the duty point, from 1 to 1,000 m³/h and up to 150 bar.

2–3year payback

Savings that fund it

Energy savings typically recover the investment within 2–3 years; long service life and minimal maintenance hold total cost of ownership down after that. Spread it with strategic leasing.

Applications

Where the kilowatts pile up.

Municipal water & irrigation

Supply networks and schemes where every pumped cubic metre is billed twice — once for power, once for the ratepayer's patience.

Building services & HVAC

Circulation, cooling towers and comfort systems that run thousands of hours a year against demand that never sits still.

Process water & fluid transfer

General-purpose transfer and process duty in industry — the unglamorous load that quietly sets the power bill.
