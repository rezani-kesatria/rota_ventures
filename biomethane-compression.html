Product Supports

Biomethane Green Compression.

Compression engineered for the gas nature actually makes — variable, wet and full of methane — so renewable biogas is captured, upgraded and moved at peak efficiency.

The Problem

The cleanest fuel on site is the one going up in smoke.

Biogas won't behave

Digester and landfill gas shifts hour by hour — methane content wanders between 45% and 99%, moisture rides along, and standard compressors choke on the variation. The gas that can't be handled gets flared, and the energy in organic waste leaves as smoke.

How We Solve

The fix is pressure that follows the gas.

Pressurise it, and it pays

Compression built for biogas holds peak efficiency across the whole composition range, capturing methane that would otherwise be released or flared — and turning waste streams into pipeline-grade renewable natural gas and vehicle fuel.

The Specification

Built around the gas you have.

98%

Maximum energy recovery

Extract up to 98% of available biogas energy for grid injection or vehicle fuel — methane that would otherwise vent or burn off becomes saleable renewable natural gas.

300bar

Pressure where it counts

Pressure ratings up to 300 bar with better than 95% mechanical efficiency, moving gas from the digester header to grid injection without a second machine in between.

45–99%

Methane compatibility

Capacity from 50 to 5,000 Nm³/h across methane contents of 45–99% — one machine line that follows the feedstock instead of fighting it.

Applications

Where the waste becomes the product.

Wastewater treatment plants

Digester gas from municipal treatment works, compressed and upgraded on the same fence line where the sludge arrives.

Landfill gas recovery

Landfill methane captured instead of flared — variable, dirty feedstock handled across the full composition range.

Agriculture & food waste

Farm digesters and food-processing waste-to-energy systems turning organic residues into revenue-grade RNG.
