Biomethane Green Compression

Optimizing renewable energy throughput with advanced compression technology designed for high-efficiency biogas capture and processing.

Description

Our Biomethane Green Compression systems are specifically engineered for the unique demands of renewable biogas processing. These advanced compressors handle the variable composition and moisture content of biogas while maintaining peak efficiency. Designed to meet the highest environmental standards, our systems maximize energy recovery from organic waste streams while minimizing operational costs and environmental impact.

Category

Product Supports · Green Energy · Compression Systems · Renewable Energy

Technical Specifications

  • Capacity Range: 50 to 5,000 Nm³/h
  • Pressure Rating: Up to 300 bar
  • Efficiency: >95% mechanical efficiency
  • Gas Compatibility: Methane content 45-99%

Benefits

  • Maximum Energy Recovery: Extract up to 98% of available biogas energy for grid injection or vehicle fuel
  • Low Maintenance: Robust design reduces downtime and operating costs
  • Emission Reduction: Capture methane that would otherwise be released or flared
  • Revenue Generation: Convert waste into valuable renewable natural gas (RNG)
  • Regulatory Compliance: Meet stringent environmental standards and renewable fuel mandates

Applications

Anaerobic digestion plants, landfill gas recovery, wastewater treatment facilities, agricultural biogas operations, food processing waste-to-energy systems, and municipal organic waste processing.
