Product Supports

Biomethane Green Compression.

Compression engineered for the gas nature actually makes — variable, wet and full of methane — so renewable biogas is captured, upgraded and moved at peak efficiency.

The Problem

The cleanest fuel on site is the one going up in smoke.

Biogas won't behave

Digester and landfill gas shifts hour by hour — methane content wanders between 45% and 99%, moisture rides along, and standard compressors choke on the variation. The gas that can't be handled gets flared, and the energy in organic waste leaves as smoke.

Pressurise it, and it pays

Compression built for biogas holds peak efficiency across the whole composition range, capturing methane that would otherwise be released or flared — and turning waste streams into pipeline-grade renewable natural gas and vehicle fuel.

The Specification

Built around the gas you have.

98%

Maximum energy recovery

Extract up to 98% of available biogas energy for grid injection or vehicle fuel — methane that would otherwise vent or burn off becomes saleable renewable natural gas.

300bar

Pressure where it counts

Pressure ratings up to 300 bar with better than 95% mechanical efficiency, moving gas from the digester header to grid injection without a second machine in between.

45–99%

Methane compatibility

Capacity from 50 to 5,000 Nm³/h across methane contents of 45–99% — one machine line that follows the feedstock instead of fighting it.

Delivery

From flare tip to fill pipe.

01

Gas assessment

The feedstock is profiled before any machine is proposed.

  • Gas composition and moisture analysis across the production cycle
  • Flow and pressure profiling at the digester or landfill header
  • Downstream target review — grid, CNG fleet or on-site power
02

System design

A compression train sized to the gas, not the catalogue.

  • Machine sizing from 50 to 5,000 Nm³/h against measured flow
  • Pressure staging up to 300 bar for the chosen outlet
  • Moisture and conditioning stages ahead of compression
03

Installation & integration

The train ties into the live plant without stopping digestion.

  • Tie-in to the existing gas holder and flare header
  • Installation by certified engineers in planned phases
  • Grid injection or CNG filling connection as chosen
04

Commissioning & testing

Gas quality and pressure are proven against specification.

  • Pressure testing to full rating with safety verification
  • Gas quality checks at the injection or filling point
  • Handover documentation and operator training
05

Monitoring & support

Remote diagnostics and preventive maintenance hold recovery at design levels.

  • Remote diagnostics and 24/7 technical support hotline
  • Preventive maintenance and rapid-deployment spare parts
  • Performance optimisation consulting as feedstock changes
